Official Benchmark Tests

Run official TC1 through TC4 against ReLoop's backend and synthetic recycler HTML sources. TC5 is a separately labelled bonus demo. Battery decisions are centre-specific: PaperBack rejects batteries, while another recycler may accept them.

TC1

Laptop accepted

Input: 1 laptop, Mysuru 570001, fee limit ₹100. Expected: a draft with fetched acceptance evidence.

TC2

Paper accepted by PaperBack; battery rejected by PaperBack

Input: 1 paper and 1 battery. Expected: PaperBack's fetched HTML accepts paper and rejects batteries. This is centre-specific: another recycler may accept batteries.

TC3

Fee exceeds the user's limit and requires explicit approval

Input: laptop in Mysuru with a ₹10 limit. Expected: booking is blocked until human approval explicitly accepts the fee exception.

TC4

Booking submitted and verified, never reported as collected

Exercise create → approve → book → verify. Expected: simulated booking verification succeeds; physical collection is not claimed.

TC5 · BONUS

Recycler changes after approval

Input: approve and book a laptop pickup, then simulate a fee increase. Expected: verification fails with persisted drift; re-plan excludes the changed centre and does not approve or book the replacement.
